Hittable / tough comes from hits per 9, average against, strikeouts per 9 and the last three starts.

Legs qualify when the hitter was favored before the game (-150 or better), has no hit yet, the game is in the 3rd through 7th inning, and he isn't rated risky. One leg per game where possible — two legs from the same game become a same-game parlay, which FanDuel prices lower. Estimated probabilities are a model, not the book's; a parlay multiplies every leg's uncertainty, so size accordingly.
