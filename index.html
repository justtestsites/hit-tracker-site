Targets: favored before the game, no hit yet, better price than pregame. Green = worth a look now. Orange = late, cold, or facing a tough arm.

Change is measured from the line's opening price. The trend shows each price step during the day.

Hittable or tough comes from hits allowed, batting average against, strikeouts, and recent starts. A reliever counts as likely out after two straight days or 30+ pitches yesterday.

A hitter qualifies when he was favored before the game (-150 or better), has no hit yet, the game is in the 3rd through 6th inning, and his spot in the order is coming up again. Player odds are FanDuel's. The percentage is our estimate of his chance to get a hit.
